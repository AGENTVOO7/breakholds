Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3-10-2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Alish Bera we collects, uses and protects your information when you play Breakhold, our real-time 1v1 castle-battle game for mobile devices (the “Game”). By installing or playing the Game, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

We only collect what we need to run the Game: to create your player account, save your progress, match you with opponents, process purchases and keep the Game fair and stable. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive a unique account ID, your Google display name and your Google account email address. We never receive your Google password.

Game data. Your username, level, experience, trophies, rank, coins, gems, unit and castle upgrades, match results, and the dates you created your account and last logged in.

Device and technical data. Device model, operating system version, language, app version, IP address, and crash and performance logs. We use this to fix bugs, prevent cheating and keep matches running smoothly.

Purchase information. If you buy items in the Game, Google Play processes the payment. We receive a purchase confirmation (order ID, item and time) so we can deliver your items. We never see or store your card or bank details.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and secure your account, and let you continue on a new device.
  • Save your progress, currencies, upgrades and purchases.
  • Run matches, matchmaking, ranked seasons and leaderboards. Your username, rank and trophies are visible to other players.
  • Deliver items you buy and restore purchases.
  • Detect cheating, fraud and abuse.
  • Fix crashes, improve performance and balance the Game.

We process this information because it is necessary to provide the Game, to meet legal obligations, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the Game safe and working well. Where the law requires consent, we will ask for it first.

4. Third-party services

The Game uses these services, which process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google) — sign-in and saving your player profile
  • Google Sign-In and Google Play Billing (Google) — signing in and in-app purchases
  • Unity (Unity Technologies) — game engine, crash and performance data

5. Sharing and retention

We share information only with the service providers above, with other players (your username, rank and match results), or when the law requires it. If the Game is sold or merged, your information may transfer to the new owner under this same policy.

We keep your account and game data while your account is active. Crash logs are kept for up to 90 days. Purchase records may be kept longer where tax or accounting law requires it.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and data at any time in the Game (Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete Account) or by emailing [contact email]. We delete your data within 30 days. Full steps and details: Delete your account.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on Google Firebase servers with access rules, so each player can only change their own profile. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in the EU/UK or CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, download or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To use these rights, email [contact email]. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the Game or the law changes. We will post the new version here and update the date above. For important changes we will also tell you in the Game.
