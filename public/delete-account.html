Delete your Breakhold account and data

You can delete your Breakhold account at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes your player profile and progress. It cannot be undone.

Option 1 — In the game

  1. Open Breakhold and sign in.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button on the main menu.
  3. Go to Account and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted and you are signed out.

Option 2 — By email (no game needed)

  1. Send an email to beraalish7@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Breakhold account”.
  2. Send it from the Google account email you use in Breakhold, or include your Player ID (shown in the game under Settings ▸ Account).
  3. We confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Account sign-in (Google account link, player ID, email address)Deleted within 30 days
Player profile: username, level, XP, trophies, rankDeleted within 30 days
Coins, gems, units, castle upgrades and other in-game itemsDeleted within 30 days (cannot be restored or refunded)
Match history and leaderboard entriesDeleted within 30 days
Server backupsDeleted automatically within 30 days after your account is removed
Crash and performance logs (no name or email)Kept for up to 90 days, then deleted
Purchase records (order ID, item, date)Kept only as long as tax and accounting laws require. Payments are handled by Google Play, which keeps its own records under Google’s privacy policy.

Delete some data without deleting your account

To delete only part of your data (for example your match history), email [contact email] and tell us what you would like removed.

Uninstalling the game does not delete your account. Read our full Privacy Policy.
